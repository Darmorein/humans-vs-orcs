# Prompt pack

## Master style anchor

```text
Original 2D isometric fantasy RTS gameplay asset. Fixed 3/4 isometric camera,
2:1 projection, warm upper-left lighting, stylized hand-painted 2D, slightly
chunky mobile-readable silhouette, subtle pixel-art influence, miniature
diorama materials matching the Living World gameplay north star. Gameplay
readability first, living-world atmosphere second, spectacle third. Genuine
transparent RGBA when isolated. No labels, logo, watermark, checkerboard,
selection ring, backdrop or direct imitation of an existing franchise.
```

## Humans modifier

```text
Warm timber, practical gray stone, deep blue cloth, shields and pennants;
organized but organic medieval-fantasy construction; restrained heroic shape
language; believable weapons and working-class tools.
```

## Orcs modifier

```text
Heavy timber, dark stone, hide, bone and iron reinforcement; red cloth accents;
asymmetrical but believable construction; powerful readable silhouettes without
oversized weapons or incoherent spikes.
```

## World modifier

```text
Naturally generated frontier landscape pieces, consistent 2:1 tile diamonds,
clear road and shore connectivity, restrained private ground patches, readable
bridge and outpost silhouettes, no square-arena feeling.
```

## UI/VFX modifier

```text
One strong noun or action per icon, readable at 32–48 CSS pixels. Painted
fantasy bevels, restrained palette, no text or panel. VFX use clean transparent
falloff and do not obscure unit silhouettes.
```

For animation prompts use the job skeleton in `ANIMATION-PRODUCTION.md` and the
approved unit PNG as the identity reference.
