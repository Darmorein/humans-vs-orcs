# Living World visual contract

## North star

The game should read as a functioning miniature world before it reads as a
collection of RTS pieces. Gameplay readability remains the first priority.

## Camera and projection

- fixed 3/4 isometric camera;
- 2:1 ground diamonds;
- approximately 35° elevation;
- light from the upper-left;
- ground-contact pivot centered between a unit's feet;
- building pivot centered on the lower footprint edge.

## Rendering language

- stylized hand-painted 2D;
- compact, slightly chunky silhouettes;
- restrained surface detail at gameplay scale;
- subtle pixel-art influence without visible pixel-grid noise;
- warm natural light and cool readable shadows;
- no baked selection rings, territory lines or UI labels.

## Humans

- warm timber, practical gray stone and deep blue cloth;
- organized but organic construction;
- shields, pennants and civic details communicate ownership;
- silhouettes favor pitched roofs, square towers and stable proportions.

## Orcs

- heavy timber, dark stone, bone, hide and iron reinforcement;
- asymmetry without structural nonsense;
- red cloth accents and sharper vertical spikes;
- silhouettes remain distinct from Human equivalents at mobile scale.

## Terrain

- world tiles use comparable 2:1 diamonds;
- paths and shores connect through explicit variants;
- props do not carry large private ground patches;
- resources are readable from silhouette and color, not UI text;
- the landscape should assemble organically rather than resemble a square arena.

## UI and VFX

- one strong noun or action per icon;
- readable at 32–48 CSS pixels;
- warm metals and wood with restrained faction-color accents;
- VFX use clean transparent falloff and must not hide unit silhouettes;
- player color remains separate from Human/Orc identity.

## Production exclusions

- no direct imitation of existing franchise assets;
- no photorealism or splash-art rendering;
- no oversized weapons or castles;
- no checkerboard painted into the pixels;
- no base64 assets, labels, logos or watermarks.
